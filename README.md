# Awesome-Digital-Asset-Management

## Top Digital Asset Management (DAM) Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Creative Asset Libraries, Brand Governance & Media Delivery*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Digital Asset Management (DAM)**. These tools centralize images, videos, documents, and creative files — providing metadata, search, versioning, and delivery for marketing, creative, and content teams.



**Examples** include Bynder, Brandfolder, Canto, Cloudinary, Acquia DAM, Aprimo, Image Relay, MediaValet, Frontify, OpenAsset, Dash, and Filecamp (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: DAM is a maturing open-source domain. **ResourceSpace**, **Phraseanet**, **UnoPim DAM**, and **Openinary** provide production-grade alternatives to commercial platforms, with strong support for self-hosting, metadata management, and media transformation.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Bynder](https://www.bynder.com/)**  

  Enterprise DAM with brand templating, creative workflow automation, and analytics. Offers a local Docker version for self-hosting with license.



- **[Brandfolder](https://brandfolder.com/)**  

  Digital asset management platform with AI-powered search, brand portals, and integrations for marketing workflows.



- **[Canto](https://www.canto.com/)**  

  DAM platform focused on ease of use, with facial recognition, custom portals, and metadata management for brand assets.



- **[Cloudinary](https://cloudinary.com/)**  

  Media management and transformation platform with URL-based image/video optimization, AI-powered tagging, and CDN delivery.



- **[Acquia DAM](https://www.acquia.com/products/dam)**  

  Enterprise DAM integrated with Drupal, offering brand portals, asset delivery, and marketing workflow tools.



- **[Aprimo](https://www.aprimo.com/)**  

  Integrated marketing operations platform with DAM, content operations, and performance analytics.



- **[Image Relay](https://www.imagerelay.com/)**  

  DAM platform with marketing asset management, brand portals, and file sharing.



- **[MediaValet](https://www.mediavalet.com/)**  

  Cloud-native DAM built for enterprise with AI, workflow automation, and brand compliance tools.



- **[Frontify](https://www.frontify.com/)**  

  Brand management platform combining DAM with brand guidelines, design templates, and collaboration tools.



- **[OpenAsset](https://www.openasset.com/)**  

  DAM specifically for architecture, engineering, and construction (AEC) firms to manage project imagery.



- **[Dash](https://www.dash.app/)**  

  DAM platform for growing brands with WordPress integration and AI-powered tagging.



- **[Filecamp](https://filecamp.com/)**  

  Digital asset management for smaller teams and agencies, with brand guidelines, file sharing, and custom portals.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[ResourceSpace](https://www.resourcespace.com/)**  

  The most mature open-source DAM, trusted since 2006 and used by organizations including Coca-Cola, Google, and Toyota . Completely free, open-source software with Docker deployment or LAMP stack installation . Features unlimited users, multilingual support, and flexible configuration for specific sectors . Available as a free 10GB cloud service or self-hosted on any platform supporting Docker .



- **[Phraseanet](https://github.com/alchemy-fr/Phraseanet)**  

  Open-source DAM solution for photos, videos, audio, and documents with 234 GitHub stars and GPLv3 license . Features Elasticsearch engine, AI-assisted batch indexing, thesaurus, metadata read/write (IPTC, XMP, DC, ID, MP3), video chaptering and subtitling, rights management, workflows, web galleries, sharing, and publishing . Docker deployment with prebuilt images available on Docker Hub .



- **[UnoPim DAM](https://github.com/unopim/unopim-digital-asset-management)**  

  Flexible open-source DAM built on Laravel for the UnoPim PIM ecosystem, MIT licensed with 10,955 GitHub stars on the parent project . Features directory and asset management with folder tree, explorer view with tabs and grid/list views, drag-and-drop uploads, zoomable image previews, video and audio players, PDF viewing, image editor with AI-generated backgrounds, shared links with expiration, directory permissions, tags, and REST API . Supports images (JPG, PNG, WEBP, SVG), documents (PDF, CSV, XLSX, DOCX), video, and audio . **The most actively developed open-source DAM for e-commerce and product content management** .



- **[Openinary](https://github.com/openinary/openinary)**  

  Self-hosted Cloudinary alternative for image and video transformation via URL parameters, AGPL-3.0 licensed with 400+ GitHub stars . Runs as a single Docker container bundling Nginx, Node.js API with FFmpeg, and Next.js admin dashboard . Connects to any S3-compatible bucket (AWS S3, Cloudflare R2, Wasabi, MinIO, DigitalOcean Spaces) . Features on-the-fly image resizing, format conversion (WebP/AVIF), video thumbnails, compression, and caching . Cloudinary-compatible syntax means existing URLs and integrations carry over without rewriting .



- **[Daminik](https://github.com/daminikhq/daminik)**  

  Simple and scalable Digital Asset Manager with built-in Content Delivery Network, 245 GitHub stars, PHP-based . Positions as "the single source of truth for all your assets" .



- **[Madek](https://github.com/broggn/Madek-hfg)**  

  Web-based archive for sharing and cataloging images, video, audio, and documents, developed by Zurich University of the Arts (ZHdK), GPLv3 licensed . Crowdsources metadata retrieval and assignment to users rather than centralizing on archival experts . Components include madek-webapp, madek-api (JSON/JSON-ROA), admin panel, and authentication service .



- **[Damselfly](https://github.com/We-Cant-Spell/Damselfly)**  

  Server-hosted DAM for photos and videos with AI face detection and recognition, keyword tagging, EXIF browsing, powerful search, and batch export, GPL-3.0 licensed . Supports full-text search across filenames, keywords, EXIF data, and face tags . Tags can be written back to image files via IPTC keywords .



- **[AtroDAM](https://github.com/atrocore/atrodam)**  

  Open-source DAM "of a new generation," 99 GitHub stars, part of the AtroCore business application platform . Integrates with MDM, PIM, and other AtroCore modules .



- **[hyper Content & Digital Asset Management Server](https://www.softwareadvice.com.au/compare/29929/141910/hypercms/vs/netx)**  

  Open-source solution enabling users to store, track, and manage an organization's digital assets, rated 4.8/5 on Software Advice . Provides brand and marketing asset management, product content management, and enterprise content management capabilities.



- **[Lychee](https://github.com/LycheeOrg/Lychee)**  

  Self-hosted photo management system and digital asset manager for uploading, organizing, and sharing photo collections, PHP-based . Features tags, nested albums, metadata extraction, image watermarking, hotlink prevention, and integrated payment gateways for selling digital images under custom licenses .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Nuxeo DAM** — Built on the Nuxeo ECM platform, Java-based, LGPL/EPL/AL licensed . Supports personal collections, workflows, publications, and plugin-based extensibility .

- **EnterMedia** — Java-based open-source DAM with personal collections, workflows, and automatic metadata extraction, LGPL licensed .

- **Tactic** — Open-source DAM with workflow support .

- **Islandora** — Built on Drupal, supports in-browser viewing of range of file types with access controls .

- **MediaEdge** — High-performance image and video processing service with responsive image delivery, adaptive video streaming, and modern format support (AVIF, AV1) .

- **Voidr Images** — Lightweight open-source alternative to Cloudinary for image management .



**Frameworks for building custom DAM solutions**: Combine **ResourceSpace** for a mature, production-proven DAM foundation with unlimited users . Use **UnoPim DAM** for e-commerce and product content management with AI tagging and Laravel extensibility . Deploy **Openinary** for Cloudinary-compatible media transformation and delivery without vendor lock-in . Integrate **Phraseanet** for rich media workflows with video chaptering and subtitling . Note that true enterprise DAM platforms with brand governance, creative automation, and deep marketing integrations remain primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong asset organization, metadata management, and media processing foundations that require integration for complete brand operations.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- DAM tools handle sensitive brand and creative assets. Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening, access controls, and backup procedures.

- Open-source DAM platforms vary significantly in maturity. Evaluate gaps in creative workflow automation, brand templating, and marketing integrations before deployment.

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong asset organization, metadata management, and media processing foundations, but enterprise brand governance and creative automation remain primarily commercial offerings.
